Questions

How do I make Clean Launcher my home screen?

Open Clean Launcher, then Settings › Default home app, and choose Clean Launcher. You can also set it in your phone's Settings › Apps › Default apps › Home app.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: icon packs from Google Play, grid and list layouts, widgets, folders, the menu glass, notification dots, backup, the edge gestures and swipe down for notifications.

Pro adds the twelve icon looks, a wallpaper per screen, automatic wallpaper changes, the list style's options, a bottom bar that shows any category, and home gestures. Every Pro feature is open for 7 days from install. Pro is a one-time purchase or a yearly subscription; the price is shown on the Pro page before you buy. Your Pro settings are kept and come back as soon as Pro is on.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

Open Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose Clean Launcher and tap Cancel. Pro stays on until the end of the paid year.

I bought Pro on another phone

Sign in to the same Google account, open Settings › Clean Launcher Pro and tap Restore purchases.

Why does the app ask for the accessibility service?

Only for the edge navigation gestures (Home, Back, Recents) and for home gestures you set to lock the screen, open Recents, take a screenshot or open the power menu. The app explains this and asks for your consent first. The service does not read anything on your screen. Everything else works without it.

System gestures stop working with another launcher on my phone

Some phones turn gesture navigation off when the home screen is not their own. Open Settings › Navigation & gestures and turn on Edge gestures: swipe up from the bottom for Home, swipe up and hold for Recents, swipe in from either side for Back. If the phone's cleanup closes the launcher, allow it to start automatically and lock it in Recents, as that page shows.

How do I use an icon pack?

Install an icon pack from Google Play, then open Settings › Icon pack and pick it. Apps the pack doesn't cover are drawn to match it.

How do I move to a new phone?

Open Settings › Backup & restore › Back up to a file, move the file to the new phone, and restore it there. Widgets are not included, so add them again after restoring.

Which phones are supported?

Android 9 and later. Blur behind the menu needs Android 12 or later; older versions dim instead.
